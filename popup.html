Data & Privacy

No candidate data leaves your browser. This extension makes zero external network calls — everything stays on this device and within SmartRecruiters.

Stored locally (chrome.storage.local) on this device only:

  • Diagnostics — text lengths, keyword match results, and the profile URL, to debug missed keywords. Raw resume text is not stored unless srDiagRawCapture is explicitly enabled for debugging. Auto-deleted after 7 days (max 20 entries).
  • Last-run results (keyword & salary) — profile URLs and match/move outcomes. Auto-deleted after 24 hours.
  • Salary screening answers are redacted from logs; only the in/out-of-budget Move action is recorded.

To erase everything now: Keyword tab → Inspect diagnostics → Clear all candidate data.
